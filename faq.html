FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose Logic Domain for capital program management software?

For 40 years, Logic Domain has been recognized as a leader in software solutions focused on helping organizations manage complex capital programs, and facility asset information. Our combination of deep industry experience, customer focus, configurable technology, dedicated support, and practical innovation continues to set us apart. Since 1986, we have remained committed to a fundamental principle: capital program management should be owner-centric, with control remaining in the hands of the owner. Guided by this principle, we combine proven industry knowledge with purpose- built software to give owners greater visibility, governance, and accountability across all stakeholders— helping them manage complex capital programs and asset information with confidence and control.

What makes Logic Domain’s capital project management software different?

We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all software. Instead of forcing our clients to adapt to rigid systems, Logic Domain delivers customized software solutions and tailored implementation services designed around your unique requirements. From workflows and processes to reporting, integrations, and system capabilities, our solutions are configured to fit the way your organization actually works—not the other way around. Our goal is simple: we do the heavy lifting, so you don’t have to.

Can Logic Domain support enterprise organizations?

Yes. Our solutions are designed to be scalable and configurable, allowing organizations to support complex workflows, multiple stakeholders, projects, facilities, and information requirements as their operations grow.

Can Logic Domain’s software accommodate our specific general ledger (GL) codes?

Yes. Logic Domain can tailor the platform to your unique general ledger structure, with the flexibility to configure precise mappings to your specific GL codes. This ensures the platform aligns seamlessly with your existing accounting processes and requirements—without requiring you to change the way you manage your general ledger.

Does Logic Domain provide AI-powered solutions?

Yes. Logic Domain incorporates AI-powered capabilities and intelligent automation to help automate tasks, streamline workflows, surface information, and support smarter decision-making.

What is Compass™ capital project management software?

Compass™ is an integrated capital project management platform that brings planning, budgeting, scheduling, cost control, forecasting, cashflow, reporting, document management, and collaboration into one centralized system. It gives project owners, project managers, and finance teams real-time visibility and control across the entire project lifecycle.

Can Compass track capital project budgets and financials over multiple fiscal periods?

Yes. Compass can track fiscal-year budgets, funding sources, expenditures, encumbrances, and financial transactions across multiple fiscal periods. It also helps organizations reconcile project activity with financial information and improve reporting and audit readiness.

What is Navigator™ facility asset and space management software

Navigator is Logic Domain’s facility asset and space management software, designed to support the broader facility lifecycle by connecting information, assets, operations, and teams. It helps organizations manage facility information and processes from development through ongoing operations and maintenance.

What makes Logic Domain’s pricing approach different?

Logic Domain is designed to be accessible and budget-friendly for organizations of all sizes. We understand that many organizations, particularly educational institutions, operate with limited resources and unique budget constraints. We believe you should pay for what fits your organization—not what doesn’t. Our customized approach allows us to align the solution, implementation, and investment with your current needs while providing room to scale as your organization grows.

What makes Logic Domain’s customer support different?

We treat support as a partnership, not a ticketing system. We provide boutique-style support with dedicated specialists who take the time to understand your organization, workflows, and needs. This allows us to provide more relevant and personalized assistance to resolve issues, and help your team succeed.
