Privacy Policy

At Logic Domain, we value your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, or inquire about our solutions and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide through our website, including:

  • Full name, email address, company or organization name, and phone number (if provided).
  • Subjects, messages, and other information submitted through contact forms or email.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage data, where collected.

2. Website Contact Forms and Email Communication

When you contact Logic Domain through our website, your name, email address, and message may be stored in our website database, email systems, or authorized communication platforms. An email notification may also be sent to our team.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, provide support, and maintain communication records. Authorized personnel may access submissions for legitimate business purposes. We do not sell personal information collected through our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to inquiries and requests for information.
  • Provide product details, demonstrations, consultations, and support.
  • Maintain business communication records.
  • Improve website functionality, performance, and security.
  • Send service updates and marketing messages where permitted by law.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Information Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Information may be stored in secure databases, email systems, or authorized service-provider environments. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share it with trusted providers supporting hosting, email delivery, security, and technical operations, subject to appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, security, and traffic analytics. Where legally required, we obtain consent for non-essential cookies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal requirements. Contact form submissions and related email messages may remain in our authorized systems until no longer needed.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, withdraw consent where relevant, or opt out of marketing messages. Contact us to submit a request.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to external services. We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies.

10. Super Intelligence (SI) and Software Services

Our software products and Super Intelligence (SI) services may process information under separate customer agreements and data protection terms. This policy primarily describes information collected through our corporate website and related communications.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as our services, practices, or legal obligations change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact Us

Have questions about our Privacy Policy or how we handle your information? Contact the Logic Domain team for assistance with privacy inquiries, data requests, or any concerns regarding your personal information.
