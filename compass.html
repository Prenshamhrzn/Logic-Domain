Building Smarter Capital Programs with Complete Control.

Modern capital projects require more than spreadsheets and disconnected tools. With real-time visibility, organizations can make smarter decisions and keep projects on schedule and within budget.

Built for project owners and managers, the platform streamlines project management with powerful features like forecasting, contract and change order management, document tracking, vendor management, timesheets, and comprehensive reporting. Deliver projects faster, reduce risks, control costs, and make smarter decisions with confidence.

Core Capabilities

Core Capabilities for Capital Project Management

Manage the financial, scheduling, operational, and reporting needs of your capital projects from one connected platform.

Budget Management

Plan and control project budgets with clear visibility into approved funds, committed costs, actual expenses, and remaining balances. Monitor budget performance in real time, compare planned and actual spending, identify variances early, and prevent unexpected cost overruns.

Schedule Management

Create and manage detailed project schedules with clear visibility into tasks, milestones, dependencies, and deadlines. Assign responsibilities, track progress, and adjust timelines as project conditions change. Use Gantt charts to identify delays, overlapping activities, and critical work early, helping teams stay coordinated and deliver each project phase on time.

Project Management

Centralize project information, responsibilities, activities, approvals, risks, and progress in one connected workspace. Assign ownership, coordinate teams, monitor project health, and keep important actions moving forward. Track issues, decisions, and status updates, and enter forecasts in real time so project managers can improve accountability, respond to risks early, and deliver projects with greater control.

Fiscal Accounting

Track fiscal-year budgets, funding sources, expenditures, encumbrances, and financial transactions in one accurate accounting view. Maintain clear records across multiple fiscal periods, monitor how funds are allocated and spent, reconcile project activity with financial data, and generate cashflows. Improve audit readiness, reporting accuracy, and compliance while giving finance teams better control over every capital project.

Plan, Assign, and Control Every Task with Gantt Charts
